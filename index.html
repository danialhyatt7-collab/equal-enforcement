Commercial Area G-10/4 — A Record of Unequal Enforcement

A fifteen-year tenancy, three years of unaddressed encroachments, and one same-day removal.

Location
Commercial Area, G-10/4, Islamabad
Period
c. 2010 – 2025
Status

Last updated

01 — Summary

The case in ninety seconds

I have leased an office in the Commercial Area of G-10/4, Islamabad, for roughly fifteen years. Through a long stretch of little or no trade I never vacated the premises, never defaulted on rent, and kept the utilities paid throughout.

As my activity began to recover, the pattern around me changed. Premises adjoining mine were vacated under one stated condition of use and then put to a different use altogether, including the same line of business I conduct. Street-facing common space nearby was taken up by a photostate machine, two roadside stalls, and a further agent, all operating without a lease of their own.

Over three years, none of that drew any enforcement action. When I placed a small, self-contained printing setup in a vehicle outside my own leased office, it was removed the same day a complaint was made — with no prior notice, no hearing, and no joint sitting.

The dates are set out below in full. What follows is a comparison of the two situations side by side, and a short statement of what I am asking the authorities to do. I am not asking for the rules to be relaxed. I am asking for them to be applied to everyone at this location on the same terms.

02 — The Parties

Who occupies what

Described by role and location only — what is physically present, and since roughly when.

The complainant
Lessee of an office inside the commercial building; rent and utilities paid continuously since c. 2010.
The right-side offices (real estate premises)
Adjoining premises vacated in 2022 on the stated condition of real estate use, then partitioned and run as an educational consultancy — the same line of business as the complainant.
The tharra operators outside the real estate offices
Two roadside stalls on street-facing common space, operating in the same line of work, without a lease of their own.
The agent operating outside the IBCC office
A further operator working from common space adjacent to the IBCC office, likewise without a lease.

03 — Timeline

What happened, in order

  1. c. 2010

    Tenancy begins. Rent and utilities are paid continuously from the outset.

  2. 2010–2022

    An extended period of little or no business. The premises are never vacated and the rent is never defaulted.

  3. 2022

    Business activity begins to recover.

  4. 2022

    The right-side offices are vacated, at the building owner's request, on the stated condition of real estate use only.

  5. 2022

    Those premises are transferred to a third party despite an agreement term barring purchase of a running business on paggri; the premises are vacated by force, then re-let for real estate use.

  6. 2022 onward

    The premises are partitioned and operated as an educational consultancy — the same line of business as the complainant.

  7. ongoing

    A photostate machine is installed outside the office on common space. Two tharras are established outside the real estate offices, operating in the same line of work. A further agent operates outside the IBCC office.

  8. 2022–2025

    No enforcement action is taken against any of the above.

  9. 2025

    The complainant places a self-contained printing setup in a vehicle outside his own leased office.

  10. 2025

    The setup is removed following a complaint by the encroaching parties. No prior notice. No hearing. No joint sitting.

04 — The Asymmetry

The same location, two standards

  Operating from inside a leased office Operating on street-facing common space
Rent paid Paid continuously for roughly fifteen years None
Utility bills Paid throughout the tenancy Not borne by the operator
Years of continuity Unbroken since c. 2010 Recent, and without formal tenure
Visibility to passing customers Set back inside the building Directly on the road frontage
Ease of access Reached through the building Immediate, at the kerb
Enforcement action taken Same-day removal on complaint None over three years

05 — What Is Being Asked

The request

  1. Inspection and determination of declared vs actual use of the adjoining premises.
  2. Removal of the encroachments, applied with the same promptness already shown. — or —
  3. A written statement of the terms on which street-facing commercial activity is permitted at this location, so the same terms may be availed equally.
  4. No further enforcement action without prior notice and a hearing.

The request is not for relaxation of the law, but for its even application.
